[Module 01 · Selection Bias & Newsvendor]

Nghich ly Dark Data & Mo hinh Newsvendor Food Truck

Doanh so ghi nhan (Observed Sales) vs Nhu cau bi che giau (Censored Dark Data)

Gia su ban van hanh mot xe banh mi Food Truck tu 11:00 den 15:00. Moi ngay ban chuan bi dinh muc 40 chiec banh. Vao luc 13:08, he thong POS thong bao ban da ban het 100% hang hoa. Nguoi quan ly truyen thong se vui mung ket luan: "Nhu cau thi truong hom nay la 40, kinh doanh hoan hao!"

Day chinh la Selection Bias (Thien lech chon lua) va Censored Demand (Nhu cau bi kiem duyet / Dark Data). Doanh so ghi nhan trong database chi la: S = \min(Q, D) = \min(40, 75) = 40

Tat ca khach hang den sau khi het hang deu phai ra ve tay khong. Nhu cau that thoat U = \max(0, D - Q) = 35 khong he de lai bat ky vet tich nao trong co so du lieu ban hang! Neu huan luyen Machine Learning truc tiep tren bien so Sales, mo hinh se lien tuc du bao thieu hut va lam te liet quy mo tang truong.

[Chep vao vo - Cornell 3 Cot] Thoi gian viet: 15 phut
Cot 1: Tu khoa / Cues

  • Censored Demand
  • Selection Bias
  • Newsvendor Model
  • Critical Fractile (F*)
Cot 2: Cong thuc / Sơ đồ
  • S = min(Q, D)
  • U = max(0, D - Q) [Dark Data]
  • Ton that co hoi: Cu * U = (p - c) * U
  • F(Q*) = Cu / (Cu + Co) = (p - c) / (p - s) = 64.3%
Cot 3: Invariant / Pitfalls
  • CAM huan luyen ML tren Doanh so (Sales)!
  • Ap dung Mo hinh Tobit hoac Survival Analysis.

[Module 02 · Defensible AI Moats]

Banh da Du lieu Kep (Compound Data Flywheel) & AI Moat

Tai sao khong co hao luy trong kho du lieu tinh: Quy trinh tac nghiep la hao luy phong thu

Nhieu doanh nghiep truyen thong tu hao rang ho so huu hang Petabytes du lieu trong kho va do la "hao luy canh tranh" (Moat). Tuy nhien, nhu giao trinh Matsuo Lab Buoi 1 khang dinh: "There is no moat in having databases." Cac mo hinh ngon ngu lon nen tang (GPT-4, Gemini, Claude) ngay cang tro nen manh me va pho bien, de dang sao chep va thuong mai hoa cac kho du lieu tinh.

Hao luy AI thuc su chi duoc thiet lap thong qua Banh da Du lieu Kep (Compound Data Flywheel): Nhung mo hinh truc tiep vao quy trinh cong viec hang ngay (Workflow Integration) -> Ghi nhan hanh vi tuong tac va sua loi cua con nguoi (Telemetry) -> Sinh ra du lieu cap doc quyen (Proprietary Data) -> Tinh chinh mo hinh mien chuyen sau (SFT/DPO Fine-Tuning) -> Mang lai trai nghiem vuot troi (Superior UX) khien nguoi dung khong the roi bo.

[SaaS Silo Truyen thong]
Luu tru co so du lieu tinh trong SQL/NoSQL. De bi danh bai khi foundation model cong cong nang cap nang luc zero-shot. Chi phi chuyen doi thap.
[Deep Workflow Flywheel]
AI hoc tu hanh dong sua loi cua chuyen gia trong luc tac nghiep. Du lieu tich luy theo cap so nhan N(k) = N0*(1+gamma)^k. Hao luy phong thu tang dan theo thoi gian.

[Module 03 · Empirical Hypothesis Cycle]

Quan ly Don le Tanpin Kanri (7-Eleven Empirical Loop)

Chu trinh kiem dinh gia thuyet tung mat hang va can bang Ton that Co hoi vs Ton that Huy bo

Chuoi cua hang tien loi 7-Eleven Nhat Ban thanh cong vuot bac nho triet ly quan tri Tanpin Kanri (Quan ly tung don vi mat hang - SKU) do CEO Toshifumi Suzuki sang lap. Thay vi nhap hang hang loat theo truc giac, moi nhan vien cua hang hoat dong nhu mot nha khoa hoc thuc nghiem: thu thap tin hieu moi truong, dat ra gia thuyet nhap hang cho tung gio, va kiem toan sai lech vao cuoi ngay.

Moi quyet dinh ton kho phai doi mat voi 2 loai ton that kinh te doi nghich:

  • Kikai Sonshitsu (Ton that co hoi): Nhap qua it hang gay het hang luc cao diem. Mat bien loi nhuan Cu = p - c va gay that vong cho nguoi tieu dung.
  • Haiki Sonshitsu (Ton that huy bo / phe thai): Nhap qua nhieu do an tuoi song (com hop, sandwich) khien hang het han su dung truoc khi ban. Mat chi phi scrap Co = c - s.
[Chep vao vo - Cornell 3 Cot] Thoi gian viet: 15 phut
Cot 1: Tu khoa / Cues

  • Kikai Sonshitsu (Opportunity Loss)
  • Haiki Sonshitsu (Waste Loss)
  • Hypothesis-Driven Restock

Cot 2: Co che / Cong thuc
  • (1) Tin hieu -> (2) Telemetry POS
  • (3) Gia thuyet SKU -> (4) Kiem toan & Sua
  • Q(alpha) = Demand + z(alpha)*sigma
  • alpha* = Cu / (Cu + Co) = 4.5 / 7.0 = 64.3%
Cot 3: Invariant / Hanh dong
  • Moi don hang la mot phep thu gia thuyet khoa hoc!
  • Kiem toan sai lech cuoi ngay la buoc bat buoc (Bayesian update).
  • Duong cong tong ton that luon co dang chu U voi diem cuc tieu tai alpha*.
