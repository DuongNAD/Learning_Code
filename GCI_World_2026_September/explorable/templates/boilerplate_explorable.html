Bien doi Du lieu Bang & Split-Apply-Combine

Truc quan hoa giam chieu du lieu tu N ve K nhom (K ≤ N), hanh vi Merge va phong ngua SettingWithCopyWarning

Trong xu ly du lieu voi Pandas, co che Split-Apply-Combine (tach nhom - tong hop - gop ket qua) la nen tang bien doi du lieu tho thanh ma tran dac trung. Khi tong hop 100 ban ghi tren 5 nhom phan biet, kich thuoc dong dau ra luon thoa man bat bien chieu giam: \text{Agg}(\text{DF}) \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times P}, \quad K = 5 \le N = 100

Viec truy cap va gan gia tri cho DataFrame phai tuan thu tuyet doi quy tac: su dung .loc (nhan) hoac .iloc (chi so nguyen vi tri). Tranh gan chuoi df[col][mask] = val vi se tao ra ban sao trung gian va kich hoat canh bao nghiem trong SettingWithCopyWarning.

[Module 02 · Optimization Dynamics]

Khong gian Ham mat mat & Gradient Descent

Khao sat be mat Loss bac hai, toc do hoc η, ma tran Hessian H va ranh gioi phan ky dao dong

Toi thieu hoa rui ro thuc nghiem (ERM) dieu huong trong so $\mathbf{w}$ theo huong am cua gradient: $\mathbf{w}_{t+1} = \mathbf{w}_t - \eta \nabla L(\mathbf{w}_t)$. Tren be mat bac hai voi do cong Hessian lon nhat $\lambda_{\max}$, thuat toan Gradient Descent co mot ranh gioi hoi tu nghiem ngat:

\eta_{\text{critical}} = \frac{2}{\lambda_{\max}(H)} = \frac{2}{4.0} = 0.50

Neu toc do hoc $\eta < \eta_{\text{critical}}$ (vi du $\eta = 0.40$), qua trinh toi uu hoa hoi tu don dieu ve tam cuc tieu. Nguoc lai, khi $\eta > \eta_{\text{critical}}$ (vi du $\eta = 0.60$), buoc nhay vuot qua ranh gioi parabol gay ra Hien tuong Dao dong Phan ky ($L \to \infty$).

[Module 03 · Deep Learning Attention]

Co che Scaled Dot-Product Attention

Giai ma ma tran Attention trong Transformer, he so chia can(d_k) va on dinh phuong sai Softmax

Trong kien truc Transformer (Vaswani et al., 2017), co che chu y duoc tinh toan dua tren ba ma tran truy van Query ($Q$), khoa Key ($K$) va gia tri Value ($V$). Tich vo huong $Q K^T$ co phuong sai ty le thuan voi so chieu vector $d_k$:

\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V, \quad \frac{1}{\sqrt{d_k}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{64}} = 0.125

Neu khong chia cho $\sqrt{d_k}$, khi $d_k$ lon ($d_k \in \{16, 64, 128\}$), cac gia tri tich vo huong se qua lon ve do lon, day ham Softmax vao vung bao hoa voi dao ham cuc nho ($\approx 0$), gay ra Vanishing Gradient. Nho he so ty le $\frac{1}{\sqrt{d_k}}$, phuong sai dau vao Softmax luon duoc bao toan chinh xac bang 1.0.

  • Scores = Q @ K.T

  • Attn = softmax(Scores / √d_k)
  • Output = Attn @ V
  • Tong moi dong Attn = 1.00
Cot 3: Python / Pitfalls
  • scale = 1.0 / math.sqrt(d_k)
  • attn = torch.matmul(Q, K.transpose(-2, -1)) * scale
  • weights = F.softmax(attn, dim=-1)
  • out = torch.matmul(weights, V)
  • Tranh quen scale lam loss NaN!
