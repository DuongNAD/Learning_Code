NumPy Multi-Dimensional Array Slicing & Memory Strides

Kho sat bo nho C-Contiguous 1D, phep chieu View vs Copy, va cong thuc anh xa dia chi byte Addr(i, j)

Nguoi lap trinh moi thuong hinh dung mang da chieu giong nhu mot danh sach long nhau (nested list). Tuy nhien, trong loi kien truc NumPy, mot ndarray thuc chat chi la mot khoi bo nho dem 1D tuyen tinh lien ke (C-Contiguous Buffer) trong RAM vat ly, di kem tieu de sieu du lieu (Metadata Header) gom: dtype, shape, va bo buoc nhay strides.

Moi phan tu toa do $(i, j)$ duoc CPU truy xuat tuc thi thong qua phep bien doi affine: \text{Addr}(i, j) = \text{BasePointer} + i \cdot S_0 + j \cdot S_1 Trong do voi kieu du lieu 64-bit (int64 hoac float64), moi phan tu chiem 8 bytes: $S_1 = 8\text{ bytes}$, va buoc nhay theo hang la $S_0 = \text{dim}_1 \times 8\text{ bytes} = 40\text{ bytes}$ (voi mang $4 \times 5$).

NumPy Broadcasting Rules & Virtual Dimension Stretching

Quy tac dong hang chieu sau (Trailing Dimensions), keo gian ao khong ton bo nho (stride = 0)

Broadcasting cho phep thuc hien cac phep toan so hoc giua hai mang co kich thuoc khac nhau ma khong can sao chep du lieu (Zero-copy arithmetic). NumPy tu dong keo gian kich thuoc bang cach dat buoc nhay stride = 0 doc theo chieu co do dai bang 1.

Quy tac dong hang (Trailing Dimension Alignment): Hai mang $A$ va $B$ tuong thich khi ta so sanh cac chieu tu phai qua trai: \text{Dieu kien: } \tilde{a}_k = \tilde{b}_k \quad \lor \quad \tilde{a}_k = 1 \quad \lor \quad \tilde{b}_k = 1 Chieu ket qua co do dai $c_k = \max(\tilde{a}_k, \tilde{b}_k)$. Neu hai chieu khac nhau va ca hai deu khac 1, NumPy se lap tuc nem ngoai le ValueError.

Vectorization Speed Benchmark Simulator & SIMD Hardware Model

Khao sat chi phi Dynamic Dispatch cua Python vs Bo chi thi song song SIMD AVX-512

Tai sao trong Khoa hoc Du lieu nguoi ta tuyet doi tranh viet vong lap for truyen thong trong Python? Do la vi ban chat phan cung: Vong lap Python thuan bi tro ngai boi co che Dynamic Typing va Boxing. Moi so nguyen trong Python la mot PyObject nang 28 bytes nam rai rac khap bo nho Heap, khien CPU lien tuc bi L1/L2 Cache Miss khi truy duoi cac con tro.

Nguoc lai, NumPy sap xep du lieu lien ke thanh cac khoi 64-byte vua khop mot dong Cache Line cua CPU. Cac bo xu ly hien dai ap dung SIMD (Single Instruction, Multiple Data - vi du AVX-512), cho phep tinh toan song song tren 8 so thuc 64-bit chi trong dung 1 chu ky dong ho CPU: T_{\text{py}}(N) \approx 62.5 \times N\text{ ns}, \quad T_{\text{np}}(N) \approx 2500 + 0.21 \times N\text{ ns} He so gia toc tang vot tu $2.5\times$ o mang nho len toi gan $300\times$ khi mang dat hang trieu phan tu!
