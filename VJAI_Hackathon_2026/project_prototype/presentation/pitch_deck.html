Slide 2 • Problem Statement

The Real Problem: Climate & Trade Dual Crisis

Vulnerability in Vietnam's core breadbaskets meets stringent Net-Zero import regulations in Japan.
Mekong Delta Salinity
  • Salinity > 4‰ intruding 50–70 km into An Giang and Can Tho rice fields.
  • Conventional continuous flooding requires 7,500 m³ water/ha.
  • Anaerobic soil bacteria produce severe methane (CH4) emissions.
Highland Over-Fertilizing
  • Central Highlands coffee farms broadcast >36 kg N/ha manually.
  • Excess nitrogen volatilizes into nitrous oxide (N2O) (265× GWP vs CO2).
  • Severe soil acidification and fertilizer waste across 600,000 ha.
Japan & EU Regulatory Wall
  • Japan GX-League and EU CBAM mandate Scope 1-3 carbon transparency.
  • Non-audited commodities face 15%–25% penalty carbon tariffs.
  • Japanese trading houses demand auditable proof before signing.
Slide 3 • Market Landscape

Existing Flaws: Why Legacy Solutions Fail

Traditional AgTech provides passive charts; carbon consulting is too slow and expensive for smallholders.
Flaw 1 Passive "Dumb" IoT Dashboards
Sensors upload graphs to web portals, but no autonomous action is taken. Farmers are left with sensor fatigue and must manually guess when to switch valves.
Flaw 2 21-Day Expensive Carbon Audits
Traditional MRV consulting costs $3,000–$5,000 per certification batch and takes 3 to 4 weeks. Smallholder cooperatives are completely excluded from green export premiums.
Flaw 3 Rampant Greenwashing Risk
Self-reported paper logs and spreadsheets lack cryptographic proof. Japanese trading houses and auditors reject unverified carbon claims.
Flaw 4 Sky-High LLM Inference Cost
Naive LLM chatbot wrappers burn $0.50–$2.00 per query with frequent hallucinations, making daily agricultural automation economically impossible.
